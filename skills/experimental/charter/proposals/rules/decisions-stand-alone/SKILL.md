# Rule: decisions stand alone

**Rule.** Every decision handed to the user defines its terms and carries lettered options, why it matters, and one marked recommendation.

**Why.** A decision is often answered in one letter, later and out of context; a question that leans on coined terms or earlier turns cannot be answered that way.

**Applies.** Any question that waits on the user, in chat or in a decision log. Exempt: yes/no confirmations of an outward action.

**Check.** Each open decision shows lettered options, a "matters because" line, one marked recommendation, and each coined term in the entry is followed by its definition in that entry.

**If it blocks you.** If you cannot recommend an option, say what information is missing and make that the question.
